## Amazon Marketplace Selling Landscape 2025-2026 Research

### Market Size & Structure [K]

**Total GMV (2025):**
- Amazon + 3P sellers: $830 billion total GMV
- First-party (Amazon): $255 billion
- Third-party marketplace: $575 billion (69% of total)

**Seller Base:**
- 1.65 million active 3P sellers globally (end of 2025)
- 165,000 new sellers registered in 2025 (down 44% from 2024) — lowest in decade
- Down 21% from 2.4M in 2021 → increased consolidation
- Over 100,000 sellers now make $1M+/year (up from 60,000 in 2021)
- Top 1.6% of sellers generate 50% of US 3P GMV

### Marketplace by Country [T]

**United States:**
- 1.1 million active sellers
- $395.6 billion in sales
- Largest marketplace globally

**India:**
- 400,000+ sellers (second-highest globally)
- ~$18 billion in revenue (2022 baseline)
- Rapid growth but lower revenue per seller (volume-driven)

**Europe (UK, Germany, others):**
- UK: 330,000+ active sellers
- Pan-European FBA network available
- Leverages single inventory distribution

**Japan, UAE, Australia:**
- Mentioned as operating regions but 2025 exact seller counts not publicly available
- UAE has 24M+ monthly visitors, expanding fulfillment infrastructure

### Income Distribution [K]

**Median vs. Average (CRITICAL DISTINCTION):**
- Median monthly revenue: $1,250 globally, $1,667 in US
- Average annual income (net profit): $30K-$75K
- BUT 70% make no more than $60K annually

**Income Tiers (% of sellers):**
- 2% earn $100K+/month
- 10% earn $25K-$250K/month
- 40% earn $1K-$25K/month
- 42% earn ≤$1K/month

**FBA-Specific:**
- Average annual revenue: $160K
- Median annual revenue: $35K

### Low-Capital Entry Methods ($100-$2000) [T]

**1. Retail Arbitrage / Online Arbitrage:**
- Profit margins: 15-50% (typically 15-30% for experienced sellers)
- Success rate: 62% of online arbitrage sellers make ~$5K/month
- Start capital: As little as $100-$500
- Entry: 25% of Amazon sellers use this method
- Average SMB (small-medium business): $11,600/month sales, ~$29K annual profit

**2. Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing):**
- No startup cost (free publishing)
- Page-read rate 2025: $0.00445/page (highest since 2020)
- Range: $150/month (low) to $20K/month (high)
- Most successful use KDP Select (Kindle Unlimited) = 60-80% of income
- January 2025: $58.6M paid to authors from Kindle Unlimited

**3. Amazon Merch on Demand:**
- Free to start (no inventory)
- Royalty per unit: $2.21 on $15.99 t-shirt, $3.77 on $19.99, $5.33 on $23.99
- Hoodies: $8-13 per unit (lower volume, high margin)
- 2026 new tier: 15%+ non-organic sales → 2x royalty ($2.44 → $4.88 per $19.99 shirt)
- Example: 167 sales, 15 returns → $804 royalties (2025 actual case)

**4. Amazon Associates (Affiliate):**
- Commission rates: 1-10% by category
- Highest: Luxury beauty (10%), Amazon Haul (7%), Amazon Games
- Bounty program: $3-25 per Prime/Audible/Music signup
- Median annual affiliate salary (US): $77,893
- Range: $35K-$126.5K
- Payout: ~60 days after month earned

**5. Amazon Handmade:**
- No listing or subscription fees
- Referral fee: 15% (minimum $1.00)
- Margin: 15-30% typical, 33% of sellers >20% margin
- 55,000+ makers earn $1M+ annually
- 61% earn $1K+/month, some >$100/day
- Creative manufacturing market: $1.2 trillion (2025)

**6. Wholesale (Small-Scale):**
- Typical MOQ: 500-1000 units
- But negotiable to 50-100 for test orders
- Capital for small batch: $500-$1000+

**Recommended Capital by Method:**
- $100-$500: retail arbitrage, online arbitrage, KDP, associates
- $500-$1000: beginner wholesale + arbitrage combo
- $1K-$5K: sustainable FBA launch with marketing budget
- $5K-$10K: aggressive FBA with 10-20% marketing allocation

### Failure Rates & Survival [T]

**First Year:**
- 20% quit within first 12 months (80% survival rate) [K]
- 68% fail within first year [T] (source variation)
- BUT 58% become profitable in first year [K]
- AND 64% become profitable within first year [T]

**"90% Failure" Claim:**
- Widely cited but LACKS verified primary source
- Likely includes longer timeframes (>2 years) and definitional variations
- More conservative verified data: 20-25% annual churn among active sellers

**Key Failure Reasons:**
1. Poor product selection (chasing trends, oversaturated niches)
2. Inadequate cost calculation (landed cost ≠ COGS, pricing to negative margin)
3. Weak PPC management (spending high, converting low)
4. Insufficient market research
5. Underestimating competition
6. Poor inventory management
7. Lack of marketing expertise

**Profitability Data:**
- Average first-year profit: $29,412 (~$2,451/month)
- 46% of sellers achieve 11-25% profit margins
- 22% reach profitability within 3 months

### Vietnamese Seller Landscape [K]

**Growth 2024-2025 (July 31 baseline):**
- Product sales: +35% YoY
- FBA shipments: +40% YoY
- Sellers hitting $1M+/year: +60% YoY (significant)
- Branded products: +40% YoY (shift from volume to brand)

**Market Dynamics:**
- 70%+ of Vietnamese businesses use FBA method
- Profitability challenge: Despite higher prices than domestic, high fees + logistics eat margins
- Primary sourcing economy: Vietnam is a major sourcing hub for other sellers

**Infrastructure:**
- Amazon Global Logistics (AGL) launched 2025
- Vietnam = 2nd country globally chosen as FBA origin point (after China)
- Direct shipping from Vietnam to US FBA fulfillment centers

**Challenges:**
- Operating fees, advertising costs, shipping/packaging/logistics are high
- Profit margins lower than sales volume suggests
- Competition increasing as more Vietnamese sellers enter

### 2025-2026 Trends [T]

**AI Tools for Sellers:**
- Amazon launched Seller Assistant (March 3, 2026) with dynamic canvas AI
- ZonGuru: AI listing optimizer (OpenAI integrated)
- SellerSnap: 24/7 competitor repricing + Buy Box prediction
- Perpetua: PPC automation (bid management, budget allocation)
- Native Amazon AI: Auto-rewrite titles, bullets, descriptions (May 2025)

**Fee Changes 2026:**
- FBA fees: +$0.08 per unit average (0.5% of item price)
- Small standard <$10: +$0.12/unit
- Small standard $10-$50: +$0.25/unit
- Small standard >$50: +$0.51/unit
- Referral fees: Unchanged from 2025
- FBA prep/labeling services: Discontinued Jan 1, 2026 (US)

**Market Consolidation:**
- New seller registrations down 44% YoY
- 21% decline in active sellers 2021-2025
- Top 100K sellers growing, small sellers exiting
- Higher barriers to profitability

### Red Flags on Commonly Cited Claims [?]

1. **"90% failure rate"** — Widely cited, NO verified primary source found. Likely marketing exaggeration. Verified data: 20-25% first-year churn, 68% fail (unverified), 80% 12-month survival (verified).

2. **"Average seller makes $300K"** — CONTRADICTED by own data distribution (42% make <$1K, 40% make $1-25K). This is top-heavy median illusion.

3. **"Anyone can start with $100"** — TECHNICALLY true for arbitrage/KDP but path to $1K+/month requires $500-1K+ and 6-12 months of learning.

4. **"Vietnamese sellers = easy profit"** — FALSE. 70% use FBA but margins are compressed by high fees. Success rate and profitability NOT higher than Western sellers.

## Key Takeaways for Decision-Making

### For $0-$500 Founder:
- Best path: Arbitrage (eBay, Walmart, Amazon) → KDP + Merch → Associates
- Realistic first-year: $2-10K revenue, <$5K net (learning phase)
- Timeline: 6-12 months before testing viability

### For $500-$2000 Founder:
- Start: Retail arbitrage + small wholesale test (50-100 units)
- Add: KDP in parallel (zero cost), Associates website
- Realistic: $10-50K revenue, $2-15K net first year

### For Vietnamese Founder (Sourcing Advantage):
- DON'T assume 70% FBA adoption = profit. Verify your specific product margin.
- Leverage: AGL shipping cost advantage (Vietnam→US direct), not sale price
- Risk: Saturated VN→Amazon pipelines in many categories (electronics, small gadgets)
- Differentiate: Brand building (40% growth) over volume resale (saturated)

### Anti-Pattern: The "Easy Money" Myth
- Most marketing targets the 42% earning <$1K/month and sells them courses
- Median seller ($1,250/mo) is 6-12 months into serious operational work
- Bottom 70% likely don't break $60K annually (including part-time)
- Time investment: 20-40 hrs/week for first year, not passive income
