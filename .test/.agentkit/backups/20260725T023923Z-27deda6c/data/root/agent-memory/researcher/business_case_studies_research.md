## Key Findings by Model

### Capital Efficiency Ranking
1. **Digital-SaaS:** $0-50K capital → $50K-300K/mo (best leverage)
2. **Content-Creator:** $0-500 capital → $8-12K/mo (zero-capital, high competition)
3. **Acquisition:** $25-50K capital → $42-96K+ exits (capital returns via multiples)
4. **Commerce-Trade:** $2-15K capital → $15-25K/mo (inventory-limited)
5. **Physical-Service:** $50-200K capital → $20-300K/mo (immobile, staffing-capped)

### Time to Profit
- **Fastest:** Laundromat, HVAC, Flippa flip (month 1, immediate upon operation)
- **Medium:** SaaS, content courses (2-4 months)
- **Slow:** Plausible Analytics (14 months), YouTube (6 months to monetization)

### Risk Profile (Highest to Lowest)
1. **Content-Creator:** Platform dependency (Substack 10% fee, YouTube algorithm), concentration risk
2. **Digital-SaaS:** Competition, product-market fit uncertainty
3. **Acquisition:** Due diligence risk, algorithm risk (SEO), exit timing
4. **Commerce-Trade:** Inventory risk, marketplace risk (Etsy/Amazon changes)
5. **Physical-Service:** Lowest (demand stable, operations predictable)

## Critical Meta-Findings

1. **SaaS narrative validated:** Bootstrapped SaaS ($0 capital) → $50K+/mo is real and repeatable
2. **Physical services stable:** Predictable 20-30% margins, capital-intensive, limited scale
3. **Content creator concentration:** 0.1% of creators earn $10K+/mo; rest make <$5K/mo (power law)
4. **Acquisition ROI:** 87% annualized (24 months) is realistic for well-executed flips
5. **Marketplace risk:** Etsy/Amazon/Substack/YouTube all take 10%+ and control distribution

## Lessons Across All Cases

- **Bootstrap timing matters:** Bootstrapped to profitability, then raise if scaling needed (not for survival)
- **Distribution > product:** Success correlates with audience/channel, not product excellence
- **Margins compress over time:** Competition forces price cuts (especially in saturated niches)
- **Founder time = real cost:** Even "zero capital" cases require 1-2 years of full-time work
- **Platform risk is underpriced:** Creators assume platforms won't change terms; they do
