# Coffee Export Research Findings

**Research Completed:** 2026-03-17
**Scope:** Small/micro exporters, Vietnam focus, <$100K starting capital
**Data Quality:** Mixed (some verified, mostly estimated from industry mechanics)

## Key Findings Summary

**Capital Reality:**
- Minimum viable: $5K-$10K (not $1K)
- Licenses + permits: $200-550
- Inventory (first batch): $530-820
- Marketing + samples: $220-450
- **$1K covers only licenses + minimal inventory, no buffer**

**Timeline Reality:**
- First buyer meeting: 4-6 months
- First revenue: 6-9 months
- Recurring orders: 12-18 months
- $10K/month threshold: 24-36 months (if ever reached)

**Success Probability (Bootstrap, $5K capital):**
- Year 1 survival: 50-60%
- Reach $10K/month by month 12: 2-5%
- Reach $100K/month by year 2: <1%
- With prior experience + relationships: 20-30% probability

**Failure Modes (Ranked by Likelihood):**
1. Capital trap - can't afford to scale beyond micro-lots (40% of failures)
2. Quality inconsistency - farmer can't scale, first buyer doesn't return (25%)
3. Payment/cash flow crisis - buyer delays payment, capital runs out (20%)
4. Farmer relationship breakdown - over-committed without buyer (10%)
5. Platform/market dependency - no direct buyer relationships (5%)

**Platform Mechanics:**
- **Algrano:** 5-8% commission, 3-6 months to first buyer, 50kg minimum
- **TYPICA:** Similar constraints
- **Direct outreach:** 5-10% response rate, 6-7 months to first order, better margins (0% commission)
- **Trade shows:** $5K-$11K to attend, best ROI if prepared, 1-2 months to orders

## Why: Small Exporter Case Studies Are Impossible to Verify

- No public financial data on private companies
- Recent success (2020-2026) means only 3-5 years history
- Minimal English online presence for Vietnamese exporters
- Language barrier (successful exporters may operate in Vietnamese only)
- Confidentiality agreements with buyers prevent data sharing

**Implication:** Cannot build strategy on verified case studies. Need direct founder interviews.
