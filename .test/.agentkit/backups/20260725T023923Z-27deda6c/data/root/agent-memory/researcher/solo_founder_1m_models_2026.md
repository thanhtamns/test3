## Key Findings

**Highest-probability path:** Niche B2B SaaS (200-300 customers at $400/month = $100K revenue). Timeline: 18-24 months cold start.

**Best models ranked by solo founder success rate:**
1. **Niche B2B SaaS** ($90K-$200K MRR proven) — Kickserv ($120K), DocuSketch ($90K), Testimonial.to ($200K)
2. **AI Image Generation** ($132K MRR proven) — Photo AI by Pieter Levels, headshot tools. Requires 50K+ social followers pre-launch.
3. **Digital Products** ($50K+ MRR proven) — Resume tools, templates. Requires aggressive SEO/content marketing.
4. **API Metered Billing** ($50K+ MRR proven) — Stripe model. Requires 1K-2K customers to hit scale.
5. **Screenshot APIs** (Not viable for solo) — Commoditized, requires massive scale or pre-existing audience.

## Unit Economics (Proven)

**Photo AI (Pieter Levels):**
- ARPU: $60-70/month
- Customers: 2,200
- Monthly costs: $13K (GPU: $12K, hosting: $40, tools: $1K)
- Gross margin: 87%
- Timeline to $100K MRR: 16 months (with 600K Twitter followers)

**B2B SaaS pattern (Bannerbear, Kickserv, DocuSketch):**
- Typical ARPU: $300-600/month
- Customer count for $100K/month: 200-300
- Gross margin: 80-85%
- Timeline to $100K/month: 18-24 months cold start (no pre-existing audience)

**Digital Products (Resume tools):**
- Price per unit: $29-99
- Customer acquisition: Organic SEO
- Gross margin: 85-90%
- Customers needed for $100K/month: 2,000+ orders
- Timeline: 6-12 months with aggressive content marketing

## Cash Flow Reality

- **SaaS recurring:** Customer pays day 1, Stripe deposits day 3, COGS is fixed monthly. Positive cash flow from day 3.
- **Digital products:** Customer pays, funds arrive 48 hours later. Zero COGS. Instant positive cash flow.
- **API metered billing:** Customer invoiced month-end, pays within 7-30 days. You float 2-week compute cost. Slower than SaaS but still positive.
- **Break-even:** Month 3-4 for well-validated SaaS (10-15 customers at $400/month).

## Critical Success Factors

1. **Vertical selection:** Must have unfair advantage (industry experience, network, solved pain personally)
2. **Customer acquisition:** $0-100 CAC via direct outreach + referrals (avoid paid ads until $50K/month)
3. **Pricing:** $299-499/month for B2B SaaS, $29-99 for digital products
4. **Margins:** 80%+ gross margin is baseline (indicates low COGS)
5. **Churn:** <5% monthly for B2B SaaS is healthy

## 18-Month Path to $100K/Month

- **Months 1-3:** MVP + validation (target: 10 beta customers at $99/month)
- **Months 4-6:** Product-market fit, expand to adjacent pain point (target: 30-50 customers)
- **Months 7-12:** Raise pricing, add integrations, case studies (target: 80-150 customers)
- **Months 13-18:** Thought leadership, partnerships, hire contractors (target: 150-300 customers)

## Red Flags (Abort If Present)

- CAC > ARPU/3 (payback >6 months)
- Churn >10%/month
- No paying customers by month 4
- First customer took >4 weeks to close (demand validation failed)
- Building features no one asked for

## Why This Is Viable in 2026

- **AI tools eliminate development bottlenecks** (Claude, GitHub Copilot accelerate solo dev)
- **No-code infrastructure is mature** (Vercel, Stripe, Zapier integrate seamlessly)
- **Distribution is democratized** (Twitter/LinkedIn beats VC introductions)
- **VC-free path is proven** (Pieter Levels, Indie Hackers case studies)
- **B2B SaaS margins stay high** (low COGS, recurring revenue)
