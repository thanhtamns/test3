## Unit Economics That Actually Work

**For $30K founder trying to reach $1M/month**:

- Break-even: 100-300 customers (depends on price point)
- CAC target: <$500 (organic search, referral, community)
- Churn tolerance: <5%/month (need 20+ month payback)
- Margin needed: 70%+ (to cover overhead + reinvestment)
- Realistic timeline: 24-36 months with no pivots

**Monthly Burn to Watch**:
- Solo founder: $3-5K/mo (living expenses only, no salary)
- With 1 contractor: $5-8K/mo
- With small team (3-5): $15-25K/mo (scales after $10K MRR)

## Mechanical Advantages (March 2026)

### AI Cost Arbitrage (Real, but compressing fast)
- GPT-4 → Claude Haiku: 92% price reduction in 12 months
- Prompt caching: 90% cost reduction on repeated context
- Implication: New AI SaaS tools already 30-40% cheaper; window closes in 6 months

### Temu's CAC Blueprint
- Referral mechanics reduce CAC from $35 → $18 (50% reduction)
- Referred users 4x more likely to refer (viral coefficient)
- Payout: 90% conversion on referral credits to cash ($50 credit = $45 payout)
- Applicable to digital products: build referral bonus into freemium tier

### Shein's Supply Chain Model (Not replicable at scale, but instructive)
- Small-batch testing: Launch 100-200 units per design (rapid feedback)
- Integrated manufacturing: Direct consumer behavior → production
- Net margin: 5% at $38B scale (thin margins × massive volume)
- Lesson: Every percentage of overhead matters; 3-5% net margin is achievable with discipline

### Duolingo's Monetization Ceiling
- 9% paid conversion rate (growing only 0.5% per year)
- 70% international users, <30% of revenue (geographic pricing needed)
- Lesson: Global freemium plays without regional pricing compress margins

## What Doesn't Work (Arbitrage Traps)

1. **TikTok Shop Dropshipping**: 15-20% total fees, 15-25% return rates, net margin only 20-30% (thin)
2. **Generic AI Tools**: 6-month window before competitors match pricing; margin evaporates
3. **Freemium International**: High churn, low ARPU, support cost kills profitability
4. **Volume Without Operations**: 5% net margin requires flawless ops; one bad quarter tanks business
