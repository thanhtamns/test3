## 1. AMAZON INFLUENCER PROGRAM (NO FOLLOWER REQUIREMENT) ⭐ START HERE

### Income Reality [K]
- **Median micro-creator:** $312/month (2026 data, up 22% YoY from $200 ceiling in 2025) [K]
- **Beginner range:** $100-500/month first 3 months [K]
- **Realistic scaling:** $300-1,200/month by month 2-3 if posting consistently [T]
- **Top 10% performers:** $7,200+/month in pure commissions [T]

### Capital Required [K]
- **Zero product cost:** Pure affiliate, storefront is curated link list
- **Time investment:** 2-4 videos/week to build traction [T]
- **Tools:** Phone camera + TikTok/YouTube (both free) [K]

### Qualification [K]
- **Follower myth:** FALSE. Amazon looks at **engagement, not follower count** [K]
- **Actual gate:** Content quality + account age (30 days typical) + engagement rate [K]
- **300 followers floor:** Community consensus, but exceptions exist [T]

### Commission Rates [K]
- **Range:** 1-20% depending on product category
- **Luxury Beauty (highest):** 10%
- **Most categories (home, toys, furniture):** 3%

### Shoppable Videos [K]
- **Storefront model:** Curated list of 3-500 products pinned to profile/landing page [K]
- **Conversion lift:** 8% → 18% after earning trust + history [T]

### Failure Modes [K]
- **No audience engagement:** Follower count = vanity metric; algorithm favors watch time [K]
- **Wrong product mix:** Recommending $5 items (math: $5 × 3% = $0.15 per sale) [T]

### Recommendation
**VIABLE for $0 capital, 6-8 week runway to $300/month.** Lowest barrier entry.

## 2. BOOK ARBITRAGE (RETAIL + FBA) ⭐ STRONGEST MODEL

### Income Proof [K]
- **Baseline:** $3,000-10,000/month sustainable (verified multiple sources) [K]
- **Used books margin:** 400%+ on individual transactions (buy $2, sell $10) [K]
- **Startup capital:** $500-2,000 typical (outside $100-300 constraint)

### Per-Unit Economics [K]
- **Per-book profit:** $7-15 average after all Amazon fees [T]
- **Weekly scanning pace:** 30-40 books/minute (experienced); 200-250/week realistic [K]
- **Time-to-first-sale:** 3-7 days (FBA shipping + listing approval) [K]

### Tooling Cost [K]
- **ScoutIQ pricing:** $44/month OR $36/month annual OR part of Seller 365 ($69/10 apps) [K]
- **Features:** eScore™ shows 6-month demand history, real-time fee calculation [K]

### Failure Modes [K]
- **68% of all Amazon sellers fail in year 1** (arbitrage hardest hit) [K]
- **Price wars:** Discover profitable book, 100 other scanners find same week, price collapse [K]
- **Hidden fee pile-up:** Inbound placement, storage, Low Inventory fees stack quickly [K]
- **Books have less return rate:** vs. 30-40% for mixed pallets, 40-60% for electronics [K]

### Recommendation
**STRONG for $500-2,000 capital. NOT viable at $100-300.** Best non-platform model if capital exists.

### Failure Modes [K]

- **Etsy cheaper:** 8.25% fee vs 15% on Amazon Handmade [K]

**MARGINAL at $100-300. Viable at $500+.** Better focus on unique designs vs. mass-produced variations.

- **Margin:** 40-60% dropship vs. 10-20% wholesale [T]

### Failure Modes [K]

- **Supply chain risk:** 2-4 week lead kills momentum [K]
- **Saturation speed:** Product goes viral → 1,000 competitors in 72 hours [K]

### Recommendation
**EXPERIMENTAL. Requires strong social intuition + risk tolerance.** Not systematic/repeatable like book arbitrage.

## 5. AMAZON WAREHOUSE CLEARANCE → BUNDLE RESALE [K - HIGH RISK]

- **Pricing:** $100-500 (low-cost), $500-1,000 (mid), $1,000+ (high-value) [K]
- **Condition:** Grade A (new), Grade B (used), Grade C (salvage/broken) [K]

### Realistic Profit Math [K]
- **Optimistic case:** $500 pallet → 15-25% return = $75-125 profit [K]
- **Loss scenario:** 40-60% damage on electronics, 50% on Grade C [K]

### Hidden Cost Killers [K]

- **Sorting labor:** 8-10 hours per pallet [K]

### Why It Fails [K]

2. **Cherry-picking:** Liquidation companies remove best items before resale [K]
3. **Unsellability:** 30-40% unsellable rate breaks math at $100-300 capital [K]

### Recommendation
**❌ NOT VIABLE at $100-300.** Break-even requires $600+ capital and storage space.
