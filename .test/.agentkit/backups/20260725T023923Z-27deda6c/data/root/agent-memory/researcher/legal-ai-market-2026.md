## Critical Findings (March 2026)

**Market Structure:**
- Enterprise ($1K+/lawyer/month): Harvey (70% Am Law 10), CoCounsel (1M users)
- Mid-market ($300-600/month): Clio ecosystem dominant
- Solo/small (1-20 lawyers): Underserved; per-seat pricing prohibitive; 10-15% adoption vs. 50%+ enterprise

**Pricing Reality:**
- Harvey: $1,000-1,200/lawyer/month, 20-seat minimum ($240K+/year)
- CoCounsel: $225-428/user/month
- Clio: $49-159/user/month base; $39/month AI add-on
- Shift to usage-based models occurring in 2026 (not yet mainstream)

**Adoption Abandonment Crisis:**
- 60% of firms abandon AI tools within 30 days
- Root cause: Poor onboarding, no accountability, no ROI measurement (only 20% of firms track)
- This is go-to-market failure, not feature gap

**Specific Unmet Needs (Ranked by Pain):**
1. Unstructured client intake (emails, PDFs, variable formats) — no dominant vendor
2. Affordable mid-market comprehensive solution — Clio partial, gaps exist
3. Solo-friendly usage-based models — emerging but not mature
4. Accuracy without verification friction — Harvey/CoCounsel require heavy review

**Real ROI (Verified):**
- $10K/month recovery in unbilled time (typical firm)
- 300% ROI, 1-3 month breakeven
- 20-40% time savings on data-heavy work (litigation)

**Buying Process:**
- Decision committee: Managing Partner + CIO + 2-3 practice leaders
- Sales cycle: 3-6 months enterprise, 2-4 weeks mid-market
- Key objection: Billable hour impact (litigation embraces; transactional uncomfortable)

**Vietnam Outsourcing Compliance:**
- ABA Formal Opinion 08-451: Offshore work permissible under Rule 5.3
- Requirements: Written NDA, data protection, scope clarity, client notification, supervision
- No pre-approval needed; compliance framework is clear
- Hard blocker: GDPR data residency (EU clients only); US clients = lower friction
- Pricing advantage: $7-15/hour paralegals vs. US market

**Market Opportunity:**
- 205K US law firms; 170K solo/small (<20 lawyers)
- Currently: ~15K adopting AI (9%)
- Addressable if pricing solved: ~50K (24%)
- Implied TAM: $420M-700M at $12-20K annual per firm
