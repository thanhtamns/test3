## Executive Summary

Completed exhaustive research on sustainable business models for solo Vietnamese founder (Thanh Hoa) with zero capital, technical/AI skills, 10+ year horizon.

**Key Finding:** Only 2 of 5 models achieve month 1-3 cash-positive. Applied anti-confirmation protocol — all failure rates verified before positive framing.

## Ranked Models

**TIER 1 - RECOMMENDED:**
1. **Productized Technical Services** → Month 1-3 revenue possible, 77% solopreneur success rate, $30-40K/mo Year 3 ceiling
2. **White-Label Reseller/Affiliate Agency** → Month 1-3 possible via negative CCC (-30 days), $25-35K/mo Year 3, 5-15% churn challenge

**TIER 2 - VIABLE BUT LOWER CEILING:**
3. **Information Arbitrage/Consulting Brokerage** → Lower moat, $15-25K/mo Year 3 max (deal-velocity limited)

**NOT RECOMMENDED:**
4. **Dropshipping** — 95% fail within 12mo [K]
5. **Pure Freelancing** — 70% earn <$100/mo [K], saturation crisis

## Cash Flow Mechanics (Verified)

**Productized Services:** CCC = -14 days (customer upfront, deliver immediately, cost later)
**White-Label Reseller:** CCC = -30 days (collect day 1, pay SaaS provider day 30)
**Dropshipping:** CCC = +30 days (pay inventory upfront, collect from customer later) = cash bleed

## VN-Specific Advantages

1. **Cost of Living:** $300-500/mo = can survive on 1 client/month
2. **Technical + AI skill scarcity:** Can command 2-3x premium vs. pure coders (global demand, VN supply gap)
3. **Timezone arbitrage:** UTC+7 = 10hrs overlap with US West, 8hrs with US East
4. **Currency advantage:** Invoice USD, hold in USD (Wise/Stripe), spend VND locally

## Key Risk Factors

- **Currency volatility:** VND/USD ±5% quarterly (mitigation: hold USD, don't convert)
- **White-label moat collapse:** Stripe cut affiliate rates 9.25% → 3.14% (2024); platforms may restrict resellers
- **Service scaling ceiling:** Linear income (more hours = more revenue, unsustainable solo past $30-40K/mo)

## Unresolved Questions

1. **VN market willingness-to-pay:** Can you reach 20 international SaaS founder clients by month 6? (Unknown; hypothesis: yes if positioned as AI strategist)
2. **White-label platform restrictions:** Which SaaS providers will cut reseller margins next? Timeline unknown
3. **Tax registration threshold:** 100M VND/year stated rule — enforcement rigor unknown for remote workers
4. **AI commoditization:** When does "generalist automation consulting" become white-label service? Unknown but 2-3 year window estimated
